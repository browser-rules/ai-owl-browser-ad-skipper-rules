FAQ

How do I import the Ad Blocker & Video Downloader Ad Skipper rule JSON?

Copy the subscription URL, open Ad Blocker & Video Downloader's Ad Skipper tab, paste the URL, and confirm.

Do I need to download the JSON file?

No. Copying the URL is the primary path. Downloading is only a manual fallback.

Can I edit imported rules?

Yes. Imported rules remain visible and editable in Ad Blocker & Video Downloader's rule management UI.

Why does automation require accessibility permission?

The local service needs accessibility permission before Ad Blocker & Video Downloader can perform supported actions.

Does this page activate Ad Skipper?

No. Ad Blocker & Video Downloader includes YouTube and YouTube Music rules. Import this JSON to add rules for other apps and ad platforms.
